# Biomorphs: Ecosystem Adaptation & Fitness Engine Specification

## 1. Overview & User Story
As a user visiting Tools Hub, I want "Biomorphs: The Evolutionary Watchmaker" to feature meaningful Darwinian selective convergence rather than aimless random trait drift. 

Each session proceduralizes an **Ecosystem Challenge** generated by combining orthogonal environmental dimensions:
1. **Terrain / Habitat** (e.g. Murky Swamp, Basalt Crags, Cloud Canopy, Glass Desert, Abyssal Trench, Crystalline Caverns)
2. **Climate / Atmosphere** (e.g. Acidic Smog, Geothermal Heat, Sub-Zero Frost, Oxygen Surge, Pitch Darkness)
3. **Ecological Threat / Pressure** (e.g. Apex Stalker Predators, Airborne Hunters, Micro-Parasites, Extreme Gravity, Resource Scarcity)

The active ecosystem defines an objective **Fitness Function** (0% to 100%) evaluating organism morphology (color camouflage, limb configuration, optical array, feelers, body armor, wings, proboscis/mandibles).

In each comparative duel, candidate specimens (**Specimen Alpha** vs. **Specimen Beta**) display their real-time **Environmental Fitness Score** along with an **Adaptive Delta** relative to the parent champion (`+8% adapted` vs `-5% regression`) and positive/negative adaptive trait tags (e.g. `+Mud camouflage`, `+Sturdy stance`, `-Vulnerable eyes in murk`).

Users can guide their lineage to master the ecosystem. When an organism achieves **>= 90% Fitness**, the tool crowns an **"Apex Organism"** milestone with an option to migrate to a new, harsher biosphere. Users can also manually adjust the 3 environmental dropdowns at any time to simulate ecological cataclysms or planetary migration.

## 2. Acceptance Criteria

### 2.1 Combinable Environmental Dimensions & Ecosystem Engine
- **AC-1 (Ecosystem Dimensions via Combinable Dropdowns):**
  - **Terrain:**
    - `Murky Swamp`: Favors mud camouflage (olive/green/dark-cyan hues 80°–160°), reduced optical exposure (1-2 eyes), multi-legged mobility (>=3 limb pairs), and larger body mass.
    - `Basalt Crags`: Favors obsidian/molten hues (0°–40° / 320°–360°), heavy limb thickness (thickness >= 4.5), sturdy compact segments, and dorsal armor.
    - `Cloud Canopy`: Favors vibrant plumage/canopy camouflage (170°–220°), lightweight segments, winged/aerofoil appendages (`wing` tip), and sensory antennae.
    - `Glass Desert`: Favors reflective solar hues (35°–70°), slender moisture-saving limbs, compact segments, and long heat-dissipating antennae.
    - `Abyssal Trench`: Favors intense bioluminescent glow (intensity >= 0.7), glowing orb lures (`glow-orb` tip), expanded eye clusters (4-6 eyes), and deep oceanic hues (210°–280°).
    - `Crystalline Caverns`: Favors prismatic patterns (dots/chevrons), elongated feelers, medium articulated limbs, and pad tips.
  - **Climate / Atmosphere:**
    - `Acidic Smog`: Rewards heavy dorsal markings/armor, protective mouthparts (`proboscis`/`mandibles`), and penalized exposed eyes.
    - `Geothermal Heat`: Rewards thermal vents/glow intensity, high symmetry, and heat-dissipating feelers.
    - `Sub-Zero Frost`: Rewards compact, thick body segments to conserve core warmth and stout limbs.
    - `Oxygen Surge`: Rewards gigantism (large body segments and extended limb reach).
    - `Pitch Darkness`: Severely penalizes non-glowing organisms, rewards bioluminescence and elongated sensory feelers.
  - **Ecological Threat / Pressure:**
    - `Apex Stalkers`: Rewards speed (>=3 limb pairs), claw/pincer tips (`claw`), and terrain-matching camouflage.
    - `Airborne Hunters`: Rewards low dorsal profile (squat segments) or defensive glide wings (`wing`).
    - `Micro-Parasites`: Rewards protective antennae tips (`bulb`/`beaded`) and tight body scaling.
    - `Extreme Gravity`: Penalizes spindly limbs and excessive segment height; rewards multi-legged squat stability.
    - `Resource Scarcity`: Rewards energy efficiency (slender limbs, compact segment morphology).
- **AC-2 (Procedural Randomization & Dropdown Controls):**
  - On New Genesis (or clicking "Roll Random Ecosystem"), a random combination of the 3 dimensions is generated.
  - The tool provides accessible dropdown selects for Terrain, Climate, and Threat in a dedicated **Ecosystem Bar** right above the duel arena.
  - Changing any dropdown immediately recalculates fitness scores for all active specimens with real-time feedback.
  - Dynamic descriptive text displays the biome summary (e.g. *"Murky Swamp with Acidic Smog under Apex Stalkers"*).

### 2.2 Anatomical Extensions
- **AC-3 (Wings / Glide Appendages):**
  - Added `'wing'` to limb tip styles, rendering feathered/membranous glide flaps on upper limb pairs in HTML5 Canvas 2D.
- **AC-4 (Cranial Apparatus / Proboscis & Mandibles):**
  - Extended head morphology with `headApparatus`: `'none' | 'proboscis' | 'mandibles'`, rendered procedural canvas elements at the head tip.

### 2.3 Fitness Scoring & Visual Feedback
- **AC-5 (Deterministic Fitness Calculation):**
  - Pure function `calculateFitness(genome, ecosystem)` evaluating morphological traits against environmental pressures, returning:
    - `score`: Integer between 0 and 100.
    - `pros`: Array of positive adaptive trait strings (e.g. `['+Swamp Camouflage', '+Multi-Legged Traction', '+Mud-Proof Eye Shielding']`).
    - `cons`: Array of negative vulnerability strings (e.g. `['-Exposed Eyes in Turbid Sludge', '-High Visibility Pigment']`).
- **AC-6 (Interactive Duel Cards with Adaptive Delta):**
  - Specimen Alpha and Specimen Beta cards prominently feature:
    - High-contrast Fitness Badge: `🌿 76% Adapted`
    - Adaptive Delta comparison relative to parent champion: `(+8% vs Parent)` in bright green or `(-4% vs Parent)` in warm amber.
    - Trait pill badges showing key pros and cons.
- **AC-7 (Apex Organism Milestone):**
  - Achieving **>= 90% Fitness** marks the specimen as an **Apex Organism**:
    - Specimen card illuminates with a radiant gold/emerald border and badge: `🏆 Apex Organism`.
    - Toast / notification celebrates the milestone and offers a button to *"Migrate to a New Biosphere"* (generating a new random ecosystem to test the organism's adaptive resilience).

### 2.4 State Persistence & Integration
- **AC-8 (Persistence):**
  - Active ecosystem configuration (terrain, climate, threat) is persisted in `localStorage` alongside genomes under key `tools_hub_biomorphs`.
- **AC-9 (Automated Regression Test Suite):**
  - Companion automated tests in `src/tools/biomorphs/Biomorphs.test.tsx` verifying:
    - Ecosystem dropdown selection and procedural generation.
    - Accurate fitness calculation across varied terrains, climates, and threats.
    - Proper rendering of wings and cranial apparatus (proboscis/mandibles).
    - Adaptive delta calculations and Apex Organism milestone triggers.
    - Migration to new biomes.
